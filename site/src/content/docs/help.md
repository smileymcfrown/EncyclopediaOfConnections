The Encyclopaedia of Connections is a Beta and is meant to grow with its readers' help. You don't
need to be an engineer: if you've made, fixed or used something, you know things we don't.

## Tell us

Each of these opens a short form on GitHub (you need a free GitHub account):

- **[Suggest a correction](https://github.com/smileymcfrown/EncyclopediaOfConnections/issues/new?template=correction.yml)**:
  something on a page or in the finder is wrong, or you can confirm something marked ⚑. Every entry
  page also has its own link, with the entry already filled in.
- **[A connection or mechanism is missing](https://github.com/smileymcfrown/EncyclopediaOfConnections/issues/new?template=missing-mechanism.yml)**:
  describe what it does, even if you don't know its name.
- **[Something isn't working](https://github.com/smileymcfrown/EncyclopediaOfConnections/issues/new?template=problem.yml)**:
  a broken link, a page that looks wrong, or the finder giving an odd answer.

## What ⚑ means

⚑ marks something nobody has confirmed yet: a fact the research could not check against a source,
a finder value that is a best guess, or an open question from the research notes. It is the best
current answer, not a known error, so "this is right, here's how I know" helps as much as a
correction. The [needs confirming](../needs-confirming/) page lists them all.

## Change it yourself

The encyclopaedia's files are on [GitHub](https://github.com/smileymcfrown/EncyclopediaOfConnections).
The [contribution guide](https://github.com/smileymcfrown/EncyclopediaOfConnections/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
says which file to edit for each kind of change, and the house style: plain English first, with the
proper term in brackets; cite your sources; never make up a web address.
