## What Claude decided (2026-10-22, owner away; D47-D49 in PROJECT.md)

- **43 new items, 410-452**, in the owner's order: paper and card, paper pop-ups, foam, clay,
  composites, glass, then drives. Nothing was dropped for size; three merges and one deferral:
  - The paper and foam agents both proposed a **tape hinge**: one entry (412), with the foam
    agent's model-plane hinge tapes in its notes.
  - The clay and foam agents both proposed **pinning**: one entry (425), for miniatures, clay,
    resin prints and foam.
  - The pop-up agent's **concertina structure** (tunnel and carousel books) is a book format more
    than a mechanism; it is a mention in the parallel-fold pop-up (419).
  - **Shelf pins and shelf supports** (447) has no entry in the 409; the glass agent found the gap
    and it is added for wood, glass and printed shelves.
- **One new family, `pop-up & movable paper mechanisms`** (*Pops up, slides or turns when a card or
  page is opened or pulled*) for the five pop-up types: they share one setting and one power
  source (opening the page), and would be scattered over motion conversion, hinges and springs
  otherwise. Every other new entry fits an existing family; the finder's "Made of" filter gives
  the view by material, so no material families (the paper agent's advice).
- The draft list's whiffletree, summing linkage and differential-as-adder are already covered by
  91 *Whippletree* and 97 *Differential*; the multi-spindle head, transfer case, fluid manifold
  and RC dual-output gearboxes are notes in the new drive entries.
- **Notes additions** the agents proposed for existing entries (e.g. magnets lose strength at
  polymer-clay baking temperatures on 111; aquarium versus mirror silicone on 227; scarf ratio on
  270; syringe outputs on one line not moving together on 300) are applied in an update pass after
  the research run, as in R23, so no entry claims content it doesn't have.
- **Side gaps found, not added** (outside this stage): a stand-alone mitre joint for wood (foam
  agent), general jewellery findings such as jump rings (clay agent).
