
# R22 scope search: the combined list (confirmed)

Date: 2026-10-03. Six web searches, one per group, each checked against all 323 existing entries.
The owner confirmed the whole list on 2026-10-03: all 86 entries, all five new families, a new
finder answer for electrical entries instead of a 16th `job` option, and no new `fits_standard`
field (standards go in aliases and text). The entries are now items 324-409 in `outline.yaml`.

**Numbers** are the final outline numbers. They follow research priority, as for earlier
additions: the five baselines first (324-328), then tier 1 (329-345), then tier 2 (346-409), so
each group's numbers below are not in a row. Existing numbers and file names are unchanged.
Tier 1 = unusual or 3D-printing, 2 = common, baseline = the plain everyday entry that others in
the family are compared with.

**Sources:** each group's full write-up, with every page the search agent opened, is in
`scope-search-2026-10-03-<group>.md` next to this file.

## 9. Rules and changes that come with the list

- **Moved, not renumbered:** 129 eccentric adjuster moves into the new adjusters family (its
  main job is adjusting a position), as 283, 288 and 289 moved into fluid power. Its number and
  file stay the same.
- **Electrical ratings:** no new field. Each electrical entry's `load` starts with a fixed
  band: signal only / up to about 3 A / up to about 30 A / above about 30 A, then extra-low
  voltage only or rated for mains.
- **Mains safety rule (new research rule):** never give mains wiring steps; describe the
  type, say what it is not allowed for, and say that mains work must follow local law.
- **Heat safety:** every welding, brazing and soldering entry carries burn, fire, fume and
  (for arc welding) UV light warnings.
- **Knots:** grouped by job, with the Ashley Book of Knots number as each knot's reference
  (cited through Wikipedia), and tying steps linked out to Animated Knots and Wikipedia. Never
  trust a home-made knot, splice or seam to hold a person.
- **Finder (run 3's tree):** the 15 fixed `job` options stay. A new first-question answer,
  "Connect wires or power", leads to the electrical family by its family key (as fluid power is
  reached). "Hold together" gains answers for pipes, walls and tripods, and the "line parts up"
  answer widens to "line up, adjust or level". The tree is re-tested once these entries are
  researched.

## 10. Left out (each search's rejects, short form)

- Catalogues: separate entries for USB, HDMI, XLR and other plug standards; screw drive types;
  screw, nail, washer and wire sizes; VESA, NEMA, servo-spline and lens-thread patterns; 19-inch
  racks; fishing-knot lists.
- Whole systems or processes: wiring diagrams, mains installation, circuit-board building
  (perfboard, PCB, reflow); weaving, knitting, embroidery; climbing anchors and rescue systems.
- Variants kept inside an entry: each wall-anchor type, each splice, each knot, each battery plug.
- Already covered: Picatinny rails and shoes (41), GoPro prongs (67), magnetic phone mounts
  (111), stick-on camera mounts (142), magic arms (65, 66), cable ties (8).
- Too basic, too niche or no usable source: plain hard stops, furniture glides, gecko adhesive,
  fluid couplings, MR dampers, decorative knots, the sheepshank.
- Medical use: sutures, surgical knots and staplers, external fixators, micromanipulators,
  dental articulators (only their reusable ideas went into notes).
- Projectile launchers: flywheel dart blasters.
- Industrial heat joining: laser, infrared, RF, explosion and friction-stir welding,
  diffusion bonding, thermite; wave and reflow soldering. Historic: lead-and-oakum pipe joints.
  Pipe thread size tables (NPT, BSP, JIC, AN): catalogues, linked to.
